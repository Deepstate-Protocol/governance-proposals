# Establish a Two-Year 30% Deepstate Inc Endowment and Investable Operating Model

This proposal establishes an endowment for Deepstate Inc by minting 300,000,000 DEEP equal to 30% of the existing 1 billion DEEP Rewarder allocation into a Sablier stream for `0x83fB2739abd9963c5341E4A176D93a7E5Ee73445`. The complete endowment unlocks linearly over exactly one year with no cliff or initial unlock, and cannot be canceled or transferred. It then makes the Minter Controller DEEP’s sole token administrator and token-level minter for 730 days, with every controlled mint creating a separate one-year linear stream that gives Deepstate Inc 30% of the mint’s combined issuance. This transparent, onchain, time-vested economic interest gives Deepstate Inc a durable operating endowment and an underwritable relationship to protocol growth, making the company a more conventionally investable entity from a venture-capital perspective.
