# Enable Rapid Deployment of New Stock Markets

This proposal appoints the Deepstate Inc address as the Rewarder Factory’s revocable operator, transfers the Router to the Governor-owned V1 Controller, and grants the Factory only the delegated minting and hook-management permissions required to launch Rewarder V2 markets for new stock-token pairs. During the Minter Controller’s 730-day issuance window, this gives the Deepstate Inc team the capacity to list and incentivize new markets rapidly while limiting successful deployments to one every three days, prohibiting replacement of existing hooks, bounding each pool-specific quantity ramp between 1,000× and 1,000,000× growth, and preserving governance control over fees, Router ownership, and operator revocation. Each new market has a fixed 1 billion DEEP maximum reward schedule, receives 150 million DEEP of initial funding, and creates a separate one-year linear Deepstate Inc stream for `64,285,714.285714285714285714` DEEP; minting the remaining 850 million DEEP would require a separate governance vote before the issuance deadline and create an additional `364,285,714.285714285714285714` DEEP stream. The operator may permanently retire a factory-created Rewarder, burning its entire remaining balance—including governance top-ups—and forfeiting every unpaid claim, after which the same pool may be launched again following the global cooldown with a fresh Rewarder, schedule, mint, and Deepstate Inc stream.
