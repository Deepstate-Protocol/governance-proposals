# Enable Rapid Deployment of New Stock Markets

This proposal appoints the Deepstate Inc address as the Rewarder Factory’s revocable operator, transfers the Router to the Governor-owned V1 Controller, and grants the Factory only the delegated minting and hook-management permissions required to launch Rewarder V2 markets for new stock-token pairs. This gives the Deepstate Inc team the capacity to list and incentivize new markets rapidly while limiting deployments to one every three days, prohibiting replacement of existing hooks, enforcing valid quantity ramps, and preserving governance control over fees, Router ownership, and operator revocation. Each new market has a fixed 1 billion DEEP maximum reward schedule but receives only 100 million DEEP of initial reward funding. The Factory cannot mint the remaining 900 million DEEP, so completing a market’s reward funding from new issuance requires a separate governance proposal and vote.
