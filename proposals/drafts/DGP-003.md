# Enable Rapid Deployment of New Stock Markets

This proposal appoints the Deepstate Inc address as the Rewarder Factory’s revocable operator, transfers the Router to the Governor-owned V1 Controller, and grants the Factory only the delegated minting and hook-management permissions required to launch Rewarder V2 markets between canonical six-decimal USDG and an 18-decimal stock token. During the Minter Controller’s 730-day issuance window, this gives the Deepstate Inc team the capacity to list and incentivize new markets rapidly while limiting deployments to one every three days and ten over the Factory’s lifetime, prohibiting replacement of existing hooks or relaunch of a retired pool, fixing the USDG quantity ramp from 1 to 1,000,000 USDG, bounding each configurable stock ramp between 1,000× and 1,000,000× growth, and preserving governance control over fees, Router ownership, operator revocation, and ownership transfers. Each market has a fixed 1 billion DEEP maximum reward schedule, receives 150 million DEEP of initial funding, and creates a separate one-year linear Deepstate Inc stream for `64,285,714.285714285714285714` DEEP; only governance can issue the remaining 850 million DEEP through an expected-rewarder-checked top-up, which creates an additional `364,285,714.285714285714285714` DEEP stream. The operator may permanently retire the exact active Rewarder, burning its entire remaining balance—including governance top-ups—and forfeiting every unpaid claim; the pool can never be relaunched through this Factory, and anyone can burn DEEP later sent directly to its retired Rewarder.
