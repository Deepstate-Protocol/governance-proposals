This proposal authorizes a one-time allocation of exactly 10 million 2DEEP to three volunteer team members who have supported Deepstate’s business-development, design, and social-media work. `0x1fb3A8192d00aDe0ddC0EEcB4D872149Eb9C4157` receives `3,333,333.333333333333333334` 2DEEP, while `0x5715d61f99487abD65D1091b5d3a46c1b2879355` and `0xAF297Fcb531Ab662D9c2076f597D1bD424C7d2dd` each receive `3,333,333.333333333333333333` 2DEEP. The 10 million 2DEEP is distributed atomically with no recipient vesting, and the allocator retains no minting authority after execution. The same transaction reduces the 2DEEP Governor quorum from 10% to 4% of the 2DEEP supply at each proposal’s voting snapshot.
